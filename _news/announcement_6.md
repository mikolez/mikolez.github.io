Our paper *PyramidalWan: On Making Pretrained Video Model Pyramidal for Efficient Inference* has been accepted at **CVPR 2026**! It presents a low-cost recipe for turning any pretrained video diffusion model into a faster, pyramidal one — no retraining from scratch, no quality loss.
