Our paper *AdapToR: Adaptive Token Reduction for Video Diffusion Transformers* has been accepted at the **CVPR 2025 2nd Workshop on Efficient and On-Device Generation (EDGE)**! It's a lightweight token reduction technique that cuts compute for video diffusion transformers by exploiting temporal redundancy — while matching or beating quality on standard benchmarks.
